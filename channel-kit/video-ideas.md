# 10 video ideas for each channel

Each idea names the RankReel format to pick in step 1 and a two-line title (the line break is where
to press Enter). Check facts, names and scores before posting, and add your own voice or opinion:
that is what keeps clip videos inside YouTube's rules.

## Wait, Really? (did-you-know facts)

All of these use Facts with voiceover. Write the script in step 4 and let the free voice read it.

| # | Title | The fact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why airplane windows / have a tiny hole | The hole balances air pressure between the window panes |
| 2 | Why honey / never goes bad | Almost no water and very acidic, so germs cannot grow in it |
| 3 | Why the sky / is blue | Air scatters blue light more than red light |
| 4 | Octopuses have / three hearts | And their blood is blue |
| 5 | Bananas are berries / strawberries are not | By the botanical definition of a berry |
| 6 | Why you can't / tickle yourself | Your brain predicts your own touch and cancels the surprise |
| 7 | Sharks are older / than trees | Sharks appeared tens of millions of years before the first trees |
| 8 | Why flamingos / are pink | The colour comes from what they eat |
| 9 | Wombats make / cube-shaped poop | Their gut shapes it into cubes |
| 10 | A day on Venus / is longer than its year | Venus spins slower than it goes round the Sun |
